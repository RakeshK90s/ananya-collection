Frequently Asked Inquiries

Can I use these wired when the battery is completely drained? expand_more

Yes! The Apex Studio features true passive acoustic bypass. Simply plug in the included 3.5mm analog cable or USB-C digital audio cable and listen indefinitely with zero latency, even with zero battery remaining.

Does LDAC high-res streaming work with iOS devices? expand_more

Apple iOS devices stream via optimized AAC at 256kbps. LDAC (up to 990kbps 24/96) is natively supported on Android 8.0+ and dedicated Hi-Res digital audio players. iOS users can enjoy uncompressed 24-bit/192kHz audio over the included USB-C cable.

What is covered under the 2-Year Domestic Warranty? expand_more

The comprehensive 2-Year warranty covers any internal electronic defects, driver degradation, ANC microphone failure, and battery performance drop beyond normal 20% wear. LuxeCart offers free doorstep pickup and return across India.
